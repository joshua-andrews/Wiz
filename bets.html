    How this is worked out

    Saved splits
    Four times a day the page saves the latest % of bets, % of money and odds for every game that hasn't started. The last save before kickoff is the one graded.
    Grading
    Final scores come from ESPN. Each side is marked won, lost or push at the saved line. Postponed games don't count.
    Needed
    The win rate that breaks even at the saved odds. At −110 that's 52.4%. A side that wins more often than it needs is making money.
    ROI
    Profit per unit staked if you'd bet 1 unit on every one.

    Favourites win more often but pay less, so raw win rate alone can't answer the question. Comparing it with the break-even rate can. A real edge is usually a few points, which takes several hundred bets to tell apart from luck.
