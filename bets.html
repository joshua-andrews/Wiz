How far the % of bets has moved since this board first saw that game today.

Percentages are within each bet, not across the whole day, and DraftKings doesn't publish how many bets were placed, so a 90% side on a small college game may be only a handful of tickets. Splits of 100/0 are hidden unless you include them. Moneylines are left out by default because heavy favourites take 95%+ of tickets almost every game; tap Moneyline or All to see them.
